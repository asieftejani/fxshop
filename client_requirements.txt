We need to create 4 custom DocTypes for ERPNext system and connect them properly to the accounting system:

1. Partner Transfers (Hawala and Reverse Hawala)
2. Exchange Transaction (includes Trading of currencies)
3. Expenses and Costs Form

    Expenses and costs are divided into two categories.

    The first category is ongoing expenses, such as electricity, water, internet, and so on.
    
    The second category is exchange business costs.
    For example, obtaining a license has a cost. Purchasing office equipment, buying a laptop, and so on are also exchange business costs.
    These costs are not related to the investors.
    A simple example: when a tenant moves into a house, they are responsible for paying electricity, water, internet, and other ongoing expenses.
    However, they are not responsible for paying the cost of buying doors, plastering, electrical wiring, or plumbing.

    Therefore, we need a field to divide expenses into two categories: Ongoing Expenses and Exchange Business Costs.
    If Ongoing Expenses is selected, the amount will be deducted from the exchange business profit.
    If Exchange Business Costs is selected, the amount will be deducted from the exchange business capital.

    We will determine ourselves whether an expense is an ongoing expense or an exchange business cost.
    Some daily expenses can be paid together at the beginning of each month.
    For example, we do not need to fill out a separate form every day for employees' lunch. At the beginning of each month,
    we give them 3,000 AFN, and the system should recognize this as a monthly expense.

    For example, if the Zaranj Branch has already filled out a form and received the lunch allowance for this month,
    the system should not allow another lunch expense form to be created for the Zaranj Branch until the current month has ended.

    The required fields are: -

    1. Expense Frequency: Daily / Monthly / Annual / One-time
    2. Expense Priority: Essential / Normal / Low Priority
    - In some critical situations, the Normal and Low Priority options can be locked.
    3. Related Branch
    4. Expense For: For example, Shop Rent, Electricity, etc.
    5. Amount
    6. Currency
    7. Paid From
    8. Upload Documents and Add Description
    9. Comment / Note

4. Investor Capital Deposit & Withdrawal Form

    The following fields are required for the Capital Entry form:

    a. Capital Entry Number
    - Automatically generated by the system.
    b. Date & Time
    - Automatically generated by the system.
    c. Investor
    - Select the investor
    d. Transaction Type
        i - Initial Capital
        ii - Additional Capital
        iii - Partial Capital Withdrawal
        iv - Full Capital Withdrawal
    e. Capital Branch
    - Select the branch where the capital is deposited/held, for example: Zahedan Branch or Kabul Branch.
    f. Cash/Bank Account
    - Select the specific cash box or bank account, for example: Bank Keshavarzi, Zahedan.
    g. Currency
    - Select the currency, for example: USD, IRR, AFN, PKR.
    h. Amount
    - Enter the capital amount, for example: 50,000 USD.
    i. Received From / Paid To
        - Wallet
        - Cash
        - Other

    Important logic for Field (i)

    The capital can be transferred in two directions:

    - The investor's/customer's wallet can be debited, and the amount can be added to the investor's capital.
    - When capital is withdrawn, the amount can be deducted from the investor's capital and added to the investor's/customer's wallet.

    The system should automatically determine the direction based on the selected Transaction Type:

    Positive transactions:

    - Initial Capital → Add capital
    - Additional Capital → Add capital

    Negative transactions:

    - Partial Capital Withdrawal → Reduce capital
    - Full Capital Withdrawal → Reduce capital

    The user should not manually select whether the transaction is positive or negative.
    The system should determine this automatically from the Transaction Type.

    Branch restriction

    The system must track the branch where each capital amount was originally deposited.

    Important: - An investor can only withdraw capital from the same branch where that capital is held.

    For example:

    - If an investor deposits 5 billion IRR in the Iran/Zahedan Branch, that capital cannot be withdrawn from the Kabul Branch.
    - If an investor deposits 50,000 USD in the Kabul Branch, that capital cannot be withdrawn from another branch.

    The system must therefore maintain the investor's capital balance separately by branch and currency.

    For partial withdrawals, the system should reduce the available capital from the corresponding branch/currency balance.
    A full withdrawal should close the available capital for that specific branch/currency.

    Future Investor Dashboard

    Later, we will create a read-only Investor Dashboard for each investor.

    The dashboard should show:

        - Total capital
        - Capital by branch
        - Capital by currency
        - All capital deposits
        - Partial and full withdrawals
        - Current available capital
        - Capital transaction history
        - Profit/Loss
        - Available profit for withdrawal
        - Previously withdrawn profit

    The dashboard should only display information.
    Investors should not be able to directly edit capital, profit, balances, or transactions from the dashboard.

5. Profit Distribution Form for Investors and Employees

    For Profit Distribution, it would be better to use IRR as the basis, because our actual capital is also in IRR.
    The company's base currency is set to USD for future purposes.
    The exchange office’s total profit and loss will be divided into two parts: 50% for the investors and 50% for the employees.
    All expenses and costs will be paid from the 50% share allocated to the employees.
    The Expenses & Costs Form should have the following fields:
    Form Number — automatic
    Date & Time — automatic
    Expense Type — Electricity / Water / Gas / Internet / Equipment Purchase / Rent / Other
    Payment Currency
    Payment Amount
    Related Branch
    Paid From — Cash / Bank / or any other account. The balance of the selected cash or bank account must be updated accordingly.
    Finally, the form should be submitted to the Owner for approval. The payment should only be allowed after the Owner approves the expense.
